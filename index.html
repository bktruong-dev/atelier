            Reference
            
The script runs again for every frame. Draw the picture for time t; scrubbing and rewinding just pick another t. Every dial is a variable you can use by name.

            

              
t, T
current time and duration, in seconds

              
duration(seconds)
length of the timeline (default 10)

              
view(radius)
how far from the centre is visible (default 2)

              
dot(x, y, z?)
a point

              
line(x1, y1, x2, y2) or line([x,y,z], [x,y,z])
a segment

              
path(points, closed?)
a curve through [[x, y, z?], …]

              
color(c)
"#86a8ff", "ink", "accent", hsl(h, s, l, a?) or r, g, b, a?

              
pointSize(px), strokeWidth(px)
sizes in screen pixels

              
Math
sin cos sqrt PI … without Math., plus TAU PHI lerp clamp range random (seeded, so it is the same every frame)

            

            
Dials: ▶ animates one at its rate (units per second), bouncing, looping or once. Type past an end to stretch the range. Use z for 3D, then drag with Orbit on (or Shift) to turn it.
